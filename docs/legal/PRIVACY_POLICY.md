# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026/07/19

This Privacy Policy explains how 9volt ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with Nino (the "Service"), a project-management system. It applies regardless of which interface you use to access the Service.

## 1. Who This Applies To

This Policy applies to:

- **Server/project administrators** who configure Nino for their fansubbing group;
- **Staff members** added to a project within Nino, whether or not they directly interact with the Service themselves;
- **Any other individual** whose information is processed by the Service in connection with project or task management.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the Service is used, we may collect:

**Identifiers**
- Discord user ID and, where available, Discord username/display name (when accessed via Discord);
- Equivalent platform identifiers for other interfaces, if applicable;
- Server/guild IDs and channel IDs, for Discord-based configuration.

**Project and Task Data**
- Project names, episode/task metadata, and staff role assignments;
- Task status, completion timestamps, and related history needed for project tracking;
- Any notes, links, or free-text content voluntarily entered into the Service by administrators or staff in connection with a project.

**Technical and Operational Data**
- Logs of commands or API requests made to the Service, for debugging, abuse prevention, and reliability purposes;
- Timestamps and basic metadata associated with those requests.

We do **not** intentionally collect sensitive personal data (e.g., health, financial, or biometric information), and users should not submit such information through the Service.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Operate core Service functionality (tracking projects, tasks, and staff assignments);
- Associate actions and assignments with the correct individual across sessions;
- Maintain, debug, and improve the reliability of the Service;
- Prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Service;
- Respond to data subject requests as described below.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the data collected by Nino for advertising or profiling unrelated to the Service's core project-management function.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For individuals in the EU/EEA/UK, our processing of personal data relies on the following legal bases under the GDPR:

- **Legitimate interests** — operating and maintaining a functional project-management tool for the group you or your project's administrators have chosen to use it with;
- **Consent** — where you or your project's administrators voluntarily provide information to the Service (e.g., joining a project as staff);
- **Performance of a task carried out in the interest of, or at the direction of, the project** — administrators direct how Nino is configured and who is added to a project.

## 5. Where and How Data Is Stored

- The Service is currently hosted on infrastructure located in the **United States**. If you are located outside the United States, including in the EU/EEA/UK, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.
- We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored data, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.
- We retain data for as long as it is needed to provide the Service, or as otherwise described in this Policy.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties except:

- With the project/server administrators who configured the Service, as part of normal Service functionality (e.g., a project owner can see staff assigned to their project);
- With service providers who help us operate the Service's infrastructure (e.g., hosting providers), under obligations consistent with this Policy;
- Where required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or governmental request;
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our users, or others.

## 7. Your Rights

If you are located in the EU/EEA/UK, or in a jurisdiction with similar rights, you have the following rights regarding your personal data processed by the Service:

- **Right of access** — you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to rectification** — you may request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")** — you may request deletion of your personal data.
- **Right to restrict or object to processing**, in certain circumstances.
- **Right to data portability**, where technically feasible.

**How to exercise these rights:** Contact us at <me [at] ninevolt [dot] moe>. We will verify your identity before fulfilling a request where necessary.

### 7.1 Right to Erasure and Project Continuity

Because Nino tracks task assignments and project history that a fansubbing project relies on to function, a request to delete your personal data may require a **replacement assignee** to preserve the integrity of ongoing project records. Specifically:

- If you request erasure and you currently hold active task or staff assignments within a project, we will ask you or a relevant project owner or equivalent administrator to designate a replacement for those assignments before, or as part of, processing your erasure request.
- Once a replacement is designated (or the assignment is otherwise resolved), we will delete or anonymize your personal identifiers from the Service, while retaining non-identifying historical/operational records necessary for the project (e.g., "a task was completed on [date]") where legally permissible.
- If no replacement is designated within a reasonable time, we will still remove your personally identifying information to the extent possible while preserving the minimum data needed for project integrity, consistent with our obligations under applicable law.

We process erasure requests without undue delay and, in any event, within the timeframe required by applicable law (e.g., one month under the GDPR, subject to permitted extensions).

## 8. Data Retention

- We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Service to the project you are associated with, or until you request erasure as described above.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by Discord in your jurisdiction, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below this age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can address it.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced through the Service or its associated channels (e.g., a Discord server or changelog). Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
